### 🚀 Commands & Formatting

**1. Initial Roll**
Rolls your starting pool (Min: 2, Max: 9 dice). Supports arithmetic (`+` / `-`).
* **Format:** `!og [number of dice]`
* **Examples:** `!og 5` or `!og 4+2`

**2. Standard Re-roll (\`r\`)**
Re-rolls non-matching dice. Requires at least a *Basic Success* (pair) to use.
* **Risk:** If it fails to improve, your initial success level is penalized by -1.
* **Format:** `!og [remaining dice] r [saved face] [initial matching count]`
* **Example:** `!og 3 r 4 2` *(keeps a pair of 4s)*

**3. Free Re-roll (\`f\`)**
Special re-roll from Feats or gear. Can be used on complete failures with **no penalty risk**.
* **Format:** `!og [remaining dice] f [saved face] [initial matching count]`
* **Example:** `!og 5 f 0 0` *(free re-roll on 0 successes)*

**4. All In (\`a\`)**
High-stakes gamble after a successful re-roll.
* **Extreme Risk:** If it fails to improve, **all successes are completely lost (wiped to 0)**.
* **Format:** `!og [remaining dice] a [saved face] [initial matching count]`
* **Example:** `!og 1 a 5 4`

---
### 🏆 The Success Ladder
* 0 - 1 Match: **Failure** 🔴
* 2 Matches: **Basic Success** 🟠
* 3 Matches: **Critical Success** 🟡
* 4 Matches: **Extreme Success** 🟢
* 5 Matches: **Impossible Success** 🔵
* 6+ Matches: **Jackpot!!!** 🟣

*Note: All rolled **1**s are automatically bolded, and the sidebar color dynamically updates to match your success level!*" -color "#00FFFF" -footer "Type !oghelp anytime to see this guide."
